Our learning promise

Structure for understanding.
Freedom to keep learning.

Choose your study time. Use a clear module path. Demonstrate what you understand before moving on.

A plan, not a weekly lock

The twelve-week plan is a suggested pace: ten learning modules, time for revision and a final examination. Active learners may finish faster or take longer. Enrolled students can open the study guides, assigned readings, videos and private practice notes immediately.

Assessment credit follows the module order. Pass each ten-question module test with 100%, complete the written work and required peer reviews, then continue. The mid-course assessment follows Module 6. The final opens once all course requirements are complete.

Work that develops your thinking

Begin each module with an original discussion of at least 300 words. End with a written response of no more than 500 words. Use an introduction, body, conclusion and APA citations and references. Your professor checks attribution, reasoning, structure, evidence and source use.

Read critically, practise deliberately and revise after useful feedback. A page view or a video click does not prove mastery. Your progress percentage records passed module checkpoints; final-exam and completion requirements are shown separately.

A small, accountable classroom

Each intake has up to six students. Only the enrolled course you select opens in your classroom. Course work and notes save separately to your student account so you can continue on phone or desktop.

Ready submissions can be paired with another consenting student in the same course and module. Nobody marks their own work. Thursday at 08:00 Nairobi time is a peer-review service checkpoint, not a weekly student deadline. When a peer is unavailable, a documented professor-review route prevents an indefinite wait; it does not grant an automatic pass.

The professor moderates discussions and peer marks, and corrections keep an audit trail. Teacher access requires the super administrator’s clearance. Student registration does not grant teaching privileges.

Clear standards

Module tests allow five attempts; after five failures, study for seven days before trying again. The final has 25 questions and an 85% pass mark. It allows one retake after failure; if both attempts fail, a seven-day study break follows.

An enrollment that never begins is closed after twelve weeks. Saved, deliberate study work counts as starting; passive page clicks do not award grades. Students may enroll again. Completion certificates recognise course learning; they are not a claim of university accreditation or a professional licence.

Our design inspiration

We combine the freedom to choose a study schedule described by Saylor’s published learning guidance ☞ with structured guides, open educational resources, discussion and assessment described in UoPeople’s study-process guide ☞. Soma na Mbiti sets its own course rules and is independent of both institutions.

Invented percentage fees for teaching. Excludes tax, minimum charges, fixed fees and spreads. Obtain an actual tariff from the provider.

02 / DIVIDEND YIELD

Turn a payout into a percentage.

Before tax and transaction costs. A dividend is not guaranteed; a price loss can outweigh dividend income.

03 / COMPOUNDING

What could different assumptions do?

A smooth hypothetical path, not a forecast. Contributions are added at month-end; the tool converts the assumed annual rate to a monthly rate. Ignores fees, taxes and inflation. Real share returns vary and can be negative.

YOUR INFORMATION

Learner information & privacy

A clear record of what this classroom asks for and why.

Your account and student number

A verified learning account receives one student number. The account name, email and learning records support sign-in, progress, assessment results and enrollment administration. Enrollment totals count verified accounts; they are not based on the highest student number.

Your basic learner profile

The profile asks for your name, country, optional county or region, learning experience, goal, preferred language and a broad occupation category. It helps the classroom understand its learning community and provide relevant support. Complete it only after reading and agreeing to the stated use. It is a basic educational profile, not identity verification for a real trading account.

Who can see it

You can view and update your own profile. Authorised student administrators can view enrollment totals and learner profiles. Other students cannot view your record. Profile details are not sent to the AI tutor.

Keeping and clearing information

Profile information stays with your account until you clear it or request an account-data change. Clear learner profile removes the additional profile fields and keeps your account, student number, passed learning, assessment history and any issued certificate. A certificate preserves the learner name and authorised signatory details recorded when it was issued. For account-data requests, contact student@somanambiti.com.

Timed assessments

The classroom records answers, question deadlines and results. Timers continue when an assessment is left or resumed on another device. Answers and explanations appear only after the attempt has ended. Assessment interface controls can discourage opening links elsewhere; they do not establish a monitored or proctored examination.
