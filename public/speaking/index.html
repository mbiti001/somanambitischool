YOUR REHEARSAL ROOM

Say it. Hear it. Improve it.

Try a 60-second introduction, a two-minute explanation or a five-minute speech. Rehearse in English, Kiswahili or a language your audience understands. An accent is part of your voice; aim for clarity.
